About Royal Decor Studios

A Mohali-based event decoration studio with one simple promise — every celebration deserves to feel beautiful, personal and unforgettable.

Our Story

We Décor Your Emotion

Royal Decor Studios was founded in Mohali with a simple belief — every family's special moment deserves to be beautiful. What began with a handful of birthday setups has grown into a full event decoration studio serving Mohali, Chandigarh, Zirakpur, Kharar, Panchkula and nearby areas.

We combine fresh flowers, premium fabrics, thoughtful lighting and creative styling to design decorations that feel personal — never generic. From an intimate anniversary dinner to a grand wedding, our team handles everything: the concept, the setup and the on-the-day coordination, so you can simply enjoy your celebration.

Our tagline says it all: we don't just decorate venues — we décor your emotion.
