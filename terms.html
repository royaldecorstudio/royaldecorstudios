Terms & Conditions

The terms that apply when you book event decoration services with Royal Decor Studio.

Last updated: October 2026

1. About Our Services

Royal Decor Studio provides event decoration services — including birthday, wedding, anniversary, baby shower, roka ceremony, engagement, car, festival, balloon and customized decoration — across Mohali, Chandigarh, Zirakpur, Kharar, Panchkula and nearby areas of Punjab, India.

2. Enquiries & Quotations

Enquiries made by phone, WhatsApp or the website form are free and carry no obligation. Quotations are customized to your event, venue, theme and budget, and are valid for the period stated in the quotation (or 15 days if not stated).

3. Bookings & Confirmation

A booking is confirmed once you approve the quotation and the advance payment is received. The event date is reserved for you only after confirmation. We recommend booking well in advance, especially for weekends and festival dates.

4. Payments

An advance payment is required to confirm a booking; the balance is payable on or before the event date. Payment details are shared directly with you on WhatsApp or phone at the time of booking.

5. Cancellations & Changes

Cancellation and refund terms are agreed at the time of booking and shared with you in writing. As decoration involves custom preparation (fresh flowers, materials and labour scheduled for your date), advance payments may be partially or fully non-refundable depending on how close the cancellation is to the event date. Date changes are accommodated whenever our schedule allows.

6. On the Event Day

We arrive at the venue at the agreed time for setup. Please ensure the venue allows decoration and provides access for our team. Any venue permissions, electricity points or parking arrangements are the responsibility of the client unless agreed otherwise.

7. Photographs

We may photograph our decoration setups for our portfolio and social media. If you prefer your event not to be photographed or shared, please tell us at the time of booking and we will respect that.

8. Liability

While we take the utmost care with our materials and installations, Royal Decor Studio is not liable for damage caused by venue conditions, weather (for outdoor setups), or actions of third parties. Any damage to our décor or materials caused by guests or venue staff may be chargeable.

9. Intellectual Property

Design concepts, themes and creative materials produced by Royal Decor Studio remain our intellectual property. Clients may not reproduce our designs commercially without written permission.

10. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Any disputes shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the courts in Mohali, Punjab.
