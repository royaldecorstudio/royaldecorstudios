ROYAL DECOR STUDIO — WEBSITE PUBLISHING

This is a static website package. It is not online until you publish it on a hosting service.

FAST FREE OPTION (Netlify Drop):
1. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Sign in / create a free Netlify account.
3. Extract this ZIP on your computer.
4. Drag the extracted folder (the folder containing index.html, about.html, css/, js/, and images/) onto the Netlify Drop page.
5. Netlify will create a public HTTPS website URL such as https://your-site-name.netlify.app.
6. Open that URL in a private/incognito window and test the pages, phone button, and WhatsApp button.
7. Save your exact live URL.

GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE:
1. Open your Google Business Profile.
2. Choose Edit profile or Contact.
3. Find Website, paste the exact live HTTPS URL from Netlify, and Save.
4. Open the live website and ensure it works before using it in Google Ads.

IMPORTANT:
- This package intentionally does not include a sitemap/canonical URL because your final live URL is not known yet.
- The Instagram link may still be a placeholder; update it to your actual Instagram profile.
- Replace sample/styling images with your own genuine event photos before representing them as your work.
- Check your real business hours and service areas before publishing.
- If you later connect a custom domain, update the site's canonical URLs and sitemap to that domain.
