Privacy Policy

Your trust matters to us. Here's how Royal Decor Studios collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who We Are

Royal Decor Studios (“we”, “us”, “our”) is an event decoration business based in Mohali, Punjab, India, serving Mohali, Chandigarh, Zirakpur, Kharar, Panchkula and nearby areas. You can reach us on WhatsApp or phone at +91 9501354811.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to share with us when making an enquiry or booking, such as:

  • Your name and contact number
  • Event details — event type, date, venue/location and approximate budget
  • Any messages, theme ideas or reference photos you send us

If you contact us through WhatsApp, phone or the enquiry form, that conversation and its details are handled by the respective platform (WhatsApp/Meta or your telecom provider) under their own privacy policies.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiries and prepare customized quotations
  • To plan, confirm and deliver decoration services for your event
  • To communicate about bookings, schedules and changes
  • To improve our services based on feedback you share

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone.

4. Sharing of Information

Your information is shared only when necessary to deliver our services — for example, sharing the venue location and event date with our decoration team so they can set up on time. We may also share information if required by law.

5. Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable care to protect your information from unauthorized access. Enquiry details are kept only as long as needed to serve you and maintain our service records, after which they are deleted or anonymized.

6. Your Rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time. Simply WhatsApp or call us on +91 9501354811 and we'll take care of it promptly.

7. Cookies & Analytics

This website currently does not use tracking cookies. If Google Analytics or similar tools are enabled in the future, this policy will be updated to explain exactly what is collected and why.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website links to third-party services such as WhatsApp and Google Maps. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those services — please review their own policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.
